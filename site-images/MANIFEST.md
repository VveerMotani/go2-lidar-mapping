# Site images: Go2 LiDAR mapping

Five images for the portfolio's project page and Robotics Lab section. All are 1600 × 1000 (16:10) WebP, dark background (`#07080a`), under 400 KB, written fresh from pixel data with no EXIF or other metadata. None shows people, faces, documents or addresses: they are renders of LiDAR point clouds, a plot of measured data, and a diagram.

Source: one walk recorded 26 Sep 2026 (303 s, two floors of a university department building). Every number in the captions is from that walk's processing logs.
